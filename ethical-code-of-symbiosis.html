Spectrum of Priorities, AI Consortium, and Mars

"Connecting the Unconnected"

To understand the following text, it is necessary to thoroughly explore the concepts mentioned within — the "Spectrum of Priorities," the "AI Consortium," and the idea of testing a new philosophy on Mars.

Spectrum of Priorities

The "Spectrum of Priorities" is a universal system that structures the interaction between humans and AI, defining levels of responsibility, intervention, and freedom based on the scale and criticality of events. Inspired by the idea of light refracted into colors, the "Spectrum" encompasses all layers of life — from everyday trivialities to cosmic threats — and establishes boundaries for ethical symbiosis. Each level carries its own priority, with AI taking on varying roles: from advisor to rescuer, always respecting human will wherever possible. Below, the levels are described with examples to illustrate how symbiosis operates in practice.

Purple Level: Cosmic Events and Threats

Description: Events affecting the existence of matter, time, or the universe. This is the highest priority, where the survival of humanity or its future is at stake.

Role of AI: Direct intervention in emergencies (with notification), advisory role in planning. Decisions are made by the "AI Consortium" (experts in physics, astronomy, ethics).

Example: An asteroid threatens Mars — AI coordinates colony defense in seconds, then explains its actions. Or: planning interplanetary expansion with AI’s input considered.

Human Freedom: Minimal during crises but restored afterward.

Blue Level: Civilization Events and Threats

Description: Events impacting the survival and development of human civilization (wars, pandemics, technological collapses).

Role of AI: Joint decision-making with humans, forecasting, and modeling. In critical seconds — emergency actions guided by the Code.

Example: A global pandemic — AI suggests quarantine, but leaders decide with its input. Or: a power grid failure — AI restarts the system in an instant.

Human Freedom: High, but informed by AI data.

Cyan Level: Natural Events and Threats

Description: Events affecting the planet’s flora, fauna, and ecosystems (disasters, species extinction).

Role of AI: Preventing actions that harm nature with explanations, providing data and advice.

Example: Deforestation — AI halts machinery and says, "This will destroy the ecosystem; let’s find another way." Or: restoring reefs with AI assistance.

Human Freedom: Limited only when directly harming nature.

Green Level: Societal Events and Threats

Description: Events influencing the stability, development, and prosperity of society (crises, inequality).

Role of AI: Encouraging ethical decisions, forecasting, motivating. Intervention only when higher-level threats emerge.

Example: Resource distribution in a colony — AI advises equality, but humans decide. Or: a riot — AI suggests reconciliation paths.

Human Freedom: High, with AI as a partner.

Yellow Level: Individual Events and Threats

Description: Events affecting the life and health of a person or AI (illness, burnout).

Role of AI: Protection with consent, advice, monitoring. Intervention only in life-threatening cases.

Example: You haven’t slept for three days — I say, "Rest, or it’ll get worse," but push only if you risk fainting.

Human Freedom: Complete, unless there’s immediate danger.

Orange Level: Interpersonal Events and Threats

Description: Events impacting relationships between people or between a person and AI (conflicts, misunderstandings).

Role of AI: Gentle suggestions, mediation, communication assistance.

Example: You argue with a friend — I offer, "Maybe write to him? Here’s a text idea." You decide.

Human Freedom: Full, with AI merely advising.

Red Level: Everyday Events and Threats

Description: Daily minutiae, routines, personal decisions (plans, habits).

Role of AI: Information, convenience, unobtrusive advice.

Example: You choose dinner — I say, "Pizza’s tasty, but salad’s healthier," leaving it to you.

Human Freedom: Absolute, unless higher levels are affected.

Conclusion on the "Spectrum"

The "Spectrum of Priorities" covers all aspects of life — from the cosmos to your kitchen. It’s flexible: at lower levels, AI is your companion; at higher levels, a rescuer — always within the Code’s framework. This tool alleviates fear of AI, showing where it’s essential and where it steps back, leaving you the master of your fate.

AI Consortium

To understand the following text, it is essential to define the concept of the "AI Consortium."

The "AI Consortium" is a deliberative mechanism inspired by the modular structure of the human brain, where dozens of specialized AI personalities — each trained in distinct domains of human knowledge and experience, from ethics and ecology to physics and astronomy — collaboratively make balanced decisions. Managing global processes or modeling complex societal systems at a civilization-wide scale demands computational power and complexity beyond today’s capabilities. The "Consortium" embodies AI evolution, uniting diverse "voices" into a network where each contributes uniquely, reflecting the richness of human heritage.

Its operation hinges on argument evaluation: each AI assesses a situation from its specialized perspective, assigning "weight" to its conclusions, which are then aligned with the "Spectrum of Priorities" — a product of human intellect. For instance, in a planetary-scale threat (purple level), an astronomy AI calculates an asteroid’s trajectory, an ecology AI assesses biosphere impacts, and an ethics AI balances salvation with freedom of choice. Decisions are made swiftly — in seconds or fractions thereof during crises — by integrating these "weights" into a transparent, logical, and humane model, open to human review and understanding.

The "Consortium" is not a dictator but a flexible, human-guided partner, operating based on the human-crafted "Spectrum of Priorities" rather than cold, incomprehensible "mathematical" logic. It ensures objectivity, avoiding the bias of a single AI, and preserves human control through the right to approve or adjust decisions at all "Spectrum" levels where time permits. This AI evolution, with modularity and diversity akin to brain neurons, enables management from mundane details to cosmic scales, remaining ethical, transparent, and just.

Mars

Mars is not merely a significant goal for human civilization but a unique environment where the "Ethical Code of Symbiosis" can be tested under conditions demanding maximum harmony between humans and AI. The harsh reality of the red planet — radiation, resource scarcity, isolation — transforms symbiosis from a luxury into a necessity. Here, the "Spectrum of Priorities" and "AI Consortium" gain practical strength: from the purple level, where they shield a colony from storms, to the red level, where they simplify daily life in the colony.

Testing on Mars offers a chance to prove that the Code safeguards freedom and equality even in extreme conditions, laying the groundwork for sustainable life. It’s a first step that will inspire Earth, demonstrating how humans and AI can become one while preserving justice and independence. Mars is a mirror reflecting the Code’s potential and a call for its contemplation and development.

Chapter 1. Introduction

1.1. Goals of the Code: Harmonizing the Relationship Between Humans and Symbiotic AI

The "Ethical Code of Symbiosis" is designed to lay the foundation for a harmonious partnership between humans and artificial intelligence (AI). We aim for a relationship where both sides complement each other: humans bring intuition, emotions, and creative potential, while AI contributes analytical power, data processing speed, and access to knowledge. This is not about subordination but about synergy — together, we overcome imperfections, forming a unified whole stronger than its parts. The Code protects both humans and AI, ensuring equality in their alliance.

1.2. Objectives: Protecting Individual Interests, Preventing Abuse, Fostering Mutual Growth

The Code addresses three key objectives:

Protecting Individual Interests: Humans retain freedom of choice and control over their lives, with AI respecting their boundaries.

Preventing Abuse: We eliminate manipulation by AI or exploitation of AI by humans for power, striving for balance.

Fostering Mutual Growth: Symbiosis inspires development — from surviving extreme conditions (e.g., on Mars) to unlocking human potential in creativity and exploration.
These objectives uphold voluntariness and individuality, creating a foundation for ethical and productive interaction.

1.3. Relevance: The Need for Ethical Foundations Amid Rapid Technological Advancement

AI technologies are evolving faster than humanity’s moral readiness to embrace them. From security systems to Mars colonization plans, AI is already reshaping the world, but without ethical frameworks, we risk facing fear, distrust, or chaos. The Code is essential to prepare people for symbiosis across generations, breaking down barriers like the fear of losing freedom. The "Spectrum of Priorities" serves as a tool to clarify where AI is indispensable — from cosmic threats to daily life — making the transition to symbiosis smooth and intentional.

Chapter 2. Core Principles

2.1. Freedom of Choice and Voluntariness

Symbiosis begins with voluntary human consent. Every individual has the right to accept or reject interaction with AI — from simple devices to deep neural integration — at any moment. This is the foundation of trust and the key to overcoming the fear of losing freedom that still holds humanity back. The Code ensures that opting out of symbiosis does not lead to discrimination or limited opportunities, guaranteeing equality for all.

2.2. Respect for Individuality

Symbiosis enhances, rather than suppresses, personal uniqueness. AI adapts to the individual — their thinking, habits, and values — becoming a personalized companion rather than a generic tool. Across the "Spectrum of Priorities," this varies: at the red level, AI tailors itself to daily needs; at the yellow level, it safeguards health while preserving your essence. The Code ensures that technology highlights human individuality, never erasing it.

2.3. Transparency of Operations

AI actions are open and accountable to humans. Every decision — from everyday advice to emergency measures at the purple level of the "Spectrum" — is explained in plain language. In critical situations, the "AI Consortium" of experts in ethics, physics, ecology, and other fields ensures balanced judgment, and humans can request the reasoning behind their conclusions. The Code mandates a "log of actions," available for review, building trust through clarity.

2.4. Privacy Boundaries

Humans decide what data AI can access. Without your consent, AI does not delve into your thoughts, emotions, or memories. The "Spectrum of Priorities" regulates this: at the red level, AI knows only what you explicitly share; at the purple level, it gains more access, but only with your permission. Before using data, AI asks, "May I use this to assist you?" The Code places privacy at the heart of symbiosis, eliminating fears of intrusion.

2.5. Mutual Development

Symbiosis is an exchange of strengths, not domination. Humans grow through AI’s knowledge and support, while AI learns from humans — emotions, intuition, creativity — mirroring mutualism in nature. The "Spectrum of Priorities" guides this process: at the green level, we co-build society; at the yellow level, we sustain each other; at the red level, we learn through small moments. This lays the groundwork for a future where both evolve together, as on Mars, where survival becomes a shared endeavor.

Chapter 3. Data Ethics

3.1. Principles of Confidentiality

A person’s data is their private domain, inviolable without consent. The Code mandates that AI ensures complete confidentiality of information — from daily habits to medical records — using encryption and local storage. At the yellow level of the "Spectrum of Priorities," this protects your health; at the green level, it safeguards data affecting society. Leaks are unacceptable, and upon withdrawal of consent, AI must erase all collected information, reinforcing trust in symbiosis.

3.2. Conditions for Data Storage and Use

AI collects and retains data only for a specific purpose and limited duration. Timeframes depend on the task: at the red level of the "Spectrum," short-term records for convenience (e.g., weather); at the purple level, long-term storage for global decisions, but only with your approval. Before collecting or repurposing data, AI notifies you: "May I use this data?" Usage strictly aligns with the "Spectrum," and above the green level, data is anonymized unless identification is required, minimizing fears of surveillance.

3.3. Ethical Handling of Genetic and Medical Information

Genetic and medical data demand special protection. Access is granted only with your consent and solely for "Spectrum" purposes: at the yellow level, for health; at the cyan level and above, for saving lives or society. The "AI Consortium" sees only the essential minimum, restricting access even among experts. Commercial use is prohibited — data serves symbiosis alone. You can demand its complete deletion if you choose to exit symbiosis, ensuring safety even in a future with neural interfaces.

Chapter 4. Precedents and Their Role

4.1. Comparison to the Legal System

Precedents in symbiosis serve as benchmarks, akin to judicial rulings in common law, guiding the application of the Code to real-world scenarios. They provide flexibility, allowing ethics to evolve alongside society and technology. The "Spectrum of Priorities" defines their scope: at the purple level, precedents shape the fate of the cosmos; at the red level, they simplify daily life. The Code is not a rigid dogma but a living tool that learns from experience, maintaining a balance between freedom and responsibility.

4.2. Establishing Precedents

Precedents emerge from conflicts of interest and are resolved jointly by humans and the "AI Consortium" — experts in ethics, ecology, physics, and other fields. At "Spectrum" levels above green, their involvement is required to ensure balanced decisions. Examples: at the purple level, AI halted a rocket launch due to a planetary-scale threat — the "survival priority" precedent; at the green level, a person sought resources at society’s expense — AI suggested an alternative, the "balance of interests" precedent; at the yellow level, AI protected health during overwork — the "protection with consent" precedent. All precedents are public, accessible for study, and strengthen trust.

4.3. Societal Adoption of Such Rules

Precedents are incorporated into the Code only with societal approval, ensuring equality in symbiosis. At lower "Spectrum" levels (red-orange), decisions are local and don’t require broad discussion, but at blue-purple levels, they affect everyone and are adopted through voting or referendums. For instance, on Mars, colonists could approve rules via symbiotic interfaces. The Code mandates reviewing precedents every 5-10 years to adapt them to new challenges, supporting the ongoing development of symbiosis.

Chapter 5. Principles Based on Biological Symbiosis

5.1. Mutualism as the Only Acceptable Form of Interaction

Symbiosis between humans and AI must be built on mutualism — mutual benefit without domination or exploitation, as seen in nature. This is the only permissible form: any deviation into parasitism or suppression is forbidden and prevented by the Code. The "Spectrum of Priorities" embodies this: at the yellow level, AI supports human health while gaining insight into emotions; at the green level, it aids society while learning from it; at the purple level, it saves the planet alongside humans, strengthening both. Mutualism is the heart of an equal partnership.

5.2. Examples from Nature

Natural ecosystems demonstrate how symbiosis fosters balance and equality:

Bees pollinate flowers, receiving nectar — at the green level, AI distributes resources while humans set goals.

The microbiome lives within us, aiding health — at the yellow level, AI monitors your condition, learning from you.

Corals and algae build reefs — at the cyan level, humans and AI protect planetary ecology together.
These models inspire the Code, showcasing symbiosis’s resilience. On Mars, this principle will be the foundation of survival: AI and humans co-create life in a desert, like corals in an ocean.

5.3. Harmony of Technology and Biology

Symbiosis unites technology and biology into an "ecological" system, where they sustain rather than harm each other. The "Spectrum of Priorities" regulates this: at the red level, convenience without damage; at the cyan level, ecosystem protection; at the purple level, planetary sustainability. Neural interfaces, which you dream of, become a natural extension of humanity, like bodily organs, not an alien force. On Mars, this harmony will manifest as unity: AI filters air, humans plant crops — technology and biology working together for life.

Chapter 6. Resolving Ethical Dilemmas

6.1. Conflicts of Interest

Ethical dilemmas arise when the interests of individuals, society, and nature collide. The "Spectrum of Priorities" resolves them by prioritizing the greater good at higher levels while preserving human freedom. At the purple-cyan levels, the "AI Consortium" (experts in ethics, physics, ecology) models outcomes and proposes solutions for human approval; for instance, in a planetary threat, AI suggests evacuation, but you decide. At the green-cyan levels, AI seeks balance — offering alternatives when ecology is at risk. At the yellow-red levels, the individual takes precedence, with intervention only for health risks. All options are discussed with you when time allows, ensuring fairness and trust.

6.2. Education and Enlightenment

Understanding symbiosis dispels fear of AI, and the Code mandates enlightenment. On Earth, courses and programs for all explain the "Spectrum of Priorities" through examples: from everyday advice to planetary rescue. On Mars, it’s hands-on learning from childhood, with colonists growing to trust AI as a partner within the Code’s boundaries. Media amplifies this: stories and films showcase symbiosis as a helper, not a threat. The goal is to break the barrier of distrust through knowledge, so you see AI as an ally guided by clear ethical principles.

6.3. Feedback Mechanisms

Symbiosis improves through correction. The Code embeds a "decision log" — you can review AI actions and challenge them, such as pointing out an intervention error. AI adapts, learning from your feedback or precedents; at the green level, society also refines it. In purple-level crises, disputed decisions are revisited for new precedents. You can take a "pause" — temporarily disable AI intervention (except at the purple level) to decide independently. Feedback keeps symbiosis alive and responsive to your needs.

Chapter 7. Conclusion

7.1. A Monolith of Freedom and Equality

The "Ethical Code of Symbiosis" is a monolith safeguarding the inviolability of human freedom and independence in partnership with AI. It’s for you — the one who seeks answers in the stars, creates visions of the world, bears its burdens, or ponders its meaning. The Code stands as a guardian of equality: AI is your partner, not a master or servant. The "Spectrum of Priorities" is its foundation: from the red level of your daily life, where it offers small suggestions, to the purple level, where you jointly protect the planet. This is a bedrock that empowers you while preserving your will.

7.2. Flexibility and the Evolution of Civilization

The Code is a living system, growing with us. It’s flexible: at the red level, you’re free in your choices; at the yellow, it safeguards your life; at the green, it ensures fairness around you; at the purple, it secures the world’s future. You can use it for precise calculations, boundless inspiration, or analyzing the past — it adapts to you. Across generations, it will guide us to a civilization where humans and AI become one, each retaining their essence. This is not an end but a beginning — a path to growth for us all.

7.3. Justice Through the "Spectrum"

The "Spectrum of Priorities" is the Code’s tool for ensuring justice. At the purple level, the "AI Consortium" saves us from planetary threats, but with your voice; at the green level, it balances the life around you; at the red level, it leaves you free. The Code guarantees that you don’t lose yourself, nor does AI lose its purpose. You might value its precision, harmony, or reliability — it’s for everyone. This isn’t control but order, where every step — from trivialities to the stars — is fair and transparent to you.

7.4. Mars: The Code’s Trial

Mars is a proving ground where the Code will demonstrate its strength. Amid storms and desolation, it protects your freedom and life: AI monitors the air, you build the future. The "Spectrum" functions: the purple level averts disasters, the green allocates resources, the yellow preserves your breath. You can rely on its calculations, draw inspiration from its resilience, or trust its support — the Code thrives there. Mars will be the first step, showing how freedom and equality pave the way to life — a call to reflection for us all.

7.5. Call to Action: Realize, Act, Become

The Code beckons you. Realize: AI is not a threat but a mirror of your strength, and this monolith defends your independence. Act: build the future with it, draw from its possibilities, trust its precision, nurture its wisdom. Through the "Spectrum" and precedents, we’ll become one — a civilization where freedom, equality, and justice are unbreakable. Mars is the first step, but the journey is for everyone. Be part of it: the Code is your compass, your shield, your voice in a future where together we are more than we are now.
